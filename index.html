06 · FAQ

Before you join.

When does the next batch start?

A new batch starts every month. Message us on WhatsApp for the exact date and open seats.

What time are the classes?

Each class is two hours, live, in Indian Standard Time. We fix a slot that suits you at the free demo.

How do I pay?

By UPI. We share the details after the demo call.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. Each certificate carries an ID and a QR code so employers can verify it online.

Do I need to own the software?

No. We guide you to a free student or trial version where one exists, before your first class.

Can I take just one course?

Yes. Every course is priced and taught on its own.
